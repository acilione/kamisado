Kamisado Rules

Goal: Move one of your towers to the opponent's home row (the row closest to them).

Setup: Each player has 8 colored towers on their home row. The board has 64 colored squares in a fixed pattern. Black moves first.

Movement

  • Towers move forward only — straight or diagonally.
  • Towers cannot jump over other pieces.
  • After a move, your opponent must move the tower whose color matches the square you landed on.
  • If that tower is blocked and cannot move, the turn passes back. If both are blocked (deadlock), the last player who moved wins the round.

Rounds & Scoring

  • A round ends when a tower reaches the opponent's home row.
  • The winning tower earns points equal to its sumo rank + 1 (minimum 1).
  • First player to reach the match target score wins.

Sumo Ranks

  • A tower that wins a round is promoted (max rank 3). The rank number is shown on the piece.
  • Rank 1 (Sumo): Max 5 spaces, can push 1 opponent piece.
  • Rank 2 (Double): Max 3 spaces, can push up to 2 pieces.
  • Rank 3 (Triple): Max 1 space, can push up to 3 pieces.
  • Pushes are straight forward only, against adjacent opponent pieces of lower rank.
  • After a push, you get another turn. The next required color is the square the pushed piece landed on.

Position Modes

  • Standard: Towers reset to the default starting positions each round.
  • Fill: After round 1, the round winner chooses to rearrange pieces by filling from the left or right based on end-of-round positions.
  • Random: Each round uses a randomly drawn position layout from 37 predefined setups.
