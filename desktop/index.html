Having trouble connecting?
  • Use the same home network. Wi-Fi and Ethernet can play together; guest or public Wi-Fi may keep devices apart.
  • On the host computer, allow Kamisado through the firewall for your trusted private network when asked.
  • For home-network play, temporarily disconnect a VPN if it blocks local devices. For remote play over a private VPN, both players must use that same VPN.
  • Keep the host app open and the computer awake. If the host reconnects or restarts the app, ask for a fresh invitation.
  • If the host has several addresses, open Connection details and choose another with “Use for invitation.” Share the updated invitation from your game.
